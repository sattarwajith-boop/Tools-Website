01

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how ToolNest Studio may collect, use, store, and protect information when you use our website and online tools.

We are committed to being transparent about how we handle data and giving you control where possible.

02

Information we may collect

  • Basic information you submit through forms, such as your name or email address
  • Usage information, browser details, device data, and general interaction metrics
  • Cookies and similar technologies used for preferences, analytics, and advertising
  • Locally stored browser data used to save drafts and preferences on your device
03

How we use information

  • To operate, improve, and secure the website and its tools
  • To respond to support messages or business inquiries
  • To understand website performance and user interests
  • To support advertising, personalization, and measurement where applicable
04

Cookies and advertising

ToolNest Studio may use cookies and related technologies for analytics, personalization, and advertising. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this or other websites.

Google may use the DoubleClick cookie or similar advertising technologies. Users may learn more about Google advertising practices and ad settings through Google's own policy and controls.

05

Third-party services

We may use third-party services such as analytics providers, advertising partners, hosting services, email tools, or embedded libraries. These services may process information according to their own privacy practices.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services you interact with through our platform.

06

Data security

We aim to use reasonable safeguards to protect information, but no method of online transmission or storage is guaranteed to be fully secure.

Most ToolNest tools process data entirely client-side in your browser, meaning your files and text never leave your device.

07

Your choices

You may control cookies through your browser settings and may avoid submitting personal information through optional forms. You may also contact us regarding privacy-related questions.

For privacy questions or requests, contact: privacy@toolneststudio.com

Privacy questions?

We're happy to answer any questions about how your data is handled. Just reach out.
