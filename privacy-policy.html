🔒 Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect information.

Last updated: May 19, 20267 sections

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how ToolNest Studio may collect, use, store and protect information when you use our website, 56+ free browser tools, contact form, and original blog guides. We aim to be transparent about data use and to keep many tool workflows browser-based where possible.

Information we may collect

We may collect basic information submitted through forms, such as your name, email address and message. We may also collect browser details, device information, usage data, cookies, analytics events, advertising identifiers, and locally stored browser preferences used by some tools.

How we use information

We use information to operate and improve ToolNest Studio, respond to support requests, understand website performance, maintain security, improve tools and guides, and support analytics or advertising where applicable.

Cookies and advertising

ToolNest Studio may use cookies and similar technologies for preferences, analytics, performance measurement, personalization and advertising. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this or other websites.

Third-party services

We may use third-party services such as hosting providers, analytics tools, advertising partners, form providers, email services, and external libraries. These services may process information according to their own privacy practices.

Data security and local tools

We use reasonable safeguards, but no online system is completely secure. Many ToolNest tools are designed to process data directly in your browser, but you should avoid entering highly sensitive information into online tools unless you understand how that page handles it.

Your choices and contact

You can control cookies through your browser settings and avoid submitting optional personal information. For privacy questions, corrections, or removal requests, contact us at toolneststudiosupport@gmail.com.

Questions about our policies?

For data or privacy-related questions, please reach out directly.
