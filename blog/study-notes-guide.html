Study Notes Guide

Good notes help students remember lessons and revise faster. A useful note is clear, short, and connected to examples. Instead of copying every sentence, write the main idea, important formula, key definition, and one example.

After class, review the note and mark weak areas. Turn the most important facts into questions. This makes revision active and helps you find mistakes before exams.

Simple method

Use headings for topics, short lines for points, and separate examples from definitions. Keep a small section for mistakes you repeat often.
