FAQ

Common questions,
clear answers.

Is ToolNest Studio completely free?

Yes — all 13+ tools are free to use with no account required. Most tools run entirely in your browser, so nothing is sent to any server.

Do my files get uploaded to your servers?

No. PDF merging, image conversion, and all text tools process your data client-side in the browser. Your data stays on your device.

Can I use these tools on mobile?

Yes, the site is responsive and works well on mobile devices. Some workspace tools with complex layouts work best on larger screens.

Will more tools be added?

Absolutely. The roadmap includes AI-enhanced tools, SEO utilities, developer tools, and more. Categories will grow as the platform scales.

Can I favorite and save tools?

Yes. Click the ♡ icon on any tool card to save it to your favorites shelf. Drafts for Resume, Budget, and Bio pages are also saved locally via localStorage.
