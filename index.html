FAQ

Design and product notes for this redesign.

Is the site fixed to light theme now?

Yes. The default experience is now light-only across the site, with accent switching still available.

Are the animations custom or library-based?

The redesign uses the installed `motion` package for reveal, stagger, and entrance animations in this static site.

Did you add a real credit card generator or AI-detection bypass?

No. This version adds a safer card validator and a natural rewrite helper instead of tools that could be used for fraud or detection evasion.

Can this become a bigger product later?

Yes. The current structure is much closer to a scalable multi-page utility platform than the earlier single-surface layout.
