Yes — all tools are free with no account required. Most tools run entirely in your browser — no server needed.

No. PDF, image, and text tools process data client-side in your browser. Files never leave your device.

Why does each tool have its own page?

Dedicated pages give each tool full space, load faster, are bookmarkable, and allow richer tool experiences.

What are the new tools added recently?

Grammar Checker, AI Detector, Plagiarism Checker, Image Collage Maker, Thumbnail Creator, AI Image Generator, Lesson Generator, Email Generator, and Credit Card Generator are all newly added.

Can I use these on mobile?

Yes — all pages are fully responsive. Complex tools like Image Collage work best on larger screens.
