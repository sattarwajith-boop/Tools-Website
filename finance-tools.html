Finance tools FAQ

Are these calculators financial advice?

No. They are general utility calculators and are not financial, tax or accounting advice.

Are exchange rates exact bank rates?

No. Currency rates are estimates from public data and banks or transfer services may use different rates and fees.

Are these tools free?

Yes. ToolNest Studio finance tools are free to use.

Which finance tool should I use first?

Use Currency Converter for exchange estimates, then Budget Calculator or Invoice Calculator depending on your task.
