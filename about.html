Who runs ToolNest Studio?

Built and maintained by Sattar Wajith and his team.

ToolNest Studio is a Sri Lanka-based web tools project created and maintained by Sattar Wajith and his team. The site was built to solve a simple problem: many people need small online tools every day, but they often have to jump between different websites, deal with sign-up walls, or use pages that provide a tool without explaining how to use it properly.

The goal of ToolNest Studio is to bring useful browser tools and practical explanations together. A student can calculate GPA, create flashcards, build a study schedule, or read a study guide. A blogger can work with meta tags, UTM links, QR codes, and content planning. A developer can format JSON, encode text, test patterns, and handle quick technical tasks. A freelancer or small business owner can use invoice, budget, percentage, and productivity tools.

The blog exists because tools alone are not always enough. Each guide explains real use cases, common mistakes, examples, and safer ways to use the tools. This helps visitors understand the task instead of only pressing a button and leaving with a result they may not fully trust.

Our Mission

We believe useful web tools should be fast, simple, clearly explained, and free to access. ToolNest Studio focuses on practical utility, beginner-friendly guidance, regular improvements, and privacy-conscious browser workflows wherever possible.
